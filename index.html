Three ways I work with data.

01

Sleep, Stress & Lifestyle Health Analysis

An observational data analysis exploring how sleep duration, sleep quality, sleep disorders, physical activity, and stress are related.

What I did

  • Conducted exploratory and nonparametric statistical analysis.
  • Used group comparisons, rank-based association methods, and visualization.
  • Interpreted results as associations rather than causal effects.

Key takeaway

  • Shorter sleep and sleep disorders were associated with higher stress.
  • Sleep duration was strongly related to sleep quality.
Nonparametric Statistics EDA Visualization
02

Predicting Customer Departure Before It Happens

A supervised machine-learning project using the UCI Iranian Churn Dataset to predict whether telecommunications customers are likely to leave.

What I did

  • Worked with 3,150 customer records and 13 predictors.
  • Compared Logistic Regression, KNN, and Random Forest.
  • Used 5-fold cross-validation and multiple classification metrics.

Key takeaway

  • Random Forest produced the strongest overall performance.
  • Best reported accuracy: 95.4%.
Machine Learning Classification Cross-Validation
03

Brain–Body AI Training Platform

A human-centered platform concept combining physical exercise, cognitive tasks, and multimodal data to support personalized training and progress tracking.

What I worked on

  • Integrated motion, heart-rate, and cognitive-task data concepts.
  • Used modeling ideas including Random Forest and LSTM.
  • Designed data-driven feedback, progress reports, and visual dashboards.

Why it matters

  • Connects analysis with an end-user product experience.
  • Shows how model outputs can be translated into actionable feedback.
